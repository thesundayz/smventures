Questions

Before you sign in

Is this a public offering?

No. The portal is only for existing shareholders of SMVC companies. Nothing on it is an offer of securities to the public.

How is the value of my shares calculated?

From the latest valuation event of each company: a funding round, an independent appraisal or book value. We always show which one, and when.

Where is my data stored?

With our hosting providers in Singapore. The privacy policy lists what we keep and how to request a copy or a correction.

Can someone else see my holdings?

Only you, people you give access to, and SMVC administrators. Every view and download is logged.
