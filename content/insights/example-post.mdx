This file shows the format of an Insights post. Everything between the two `---` lines at the top
is the frontmatter:

- **title**: the post's title, also used in search results and when it is shared.
- **date**: the publication date, written as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Posts are listed newest first.
- **summary**: one or two sentences, shown in lists, in search results and in the RSS feed.
- **lang**: `en` for the English site (`/insights`) or `id` for the Indonesian site (`/id/insights`).
- **draft**: `true` keeps the post off the live site. Change it to `false` to publish.

## Writing the body

Below the frontmatter, write in Markdown. A blank line starts a new paragraph, `##` starts a
heading, and a line starting with `-` is a list item.

Links look like [this one to the home page](https://smventures.id). **Bold** and _italics_ work
as usual.

> A line starting with `>` becomes a quote.
